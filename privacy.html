OVERRUN

Privacy Policy · last updated 28 July 2026

OVERRUN is a game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. We do not operate a server that stores anything about you, and we do not collect, sell or share personal data. This page explains the few places where data does move, so that nothing on it comes as a surprise.

What stays on your device

The game saves a small amount of state locally, using your device's browser storage. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • overrun_set – your settings (sensitivity, field of view, volume, quality).
  • overrun_best – your best survival score.
  • overrun_name – the callsign you last typed into the multiplayer lobby.

Your callsign is whatever you type. It is not verified, not linked to you, and we suggest you do not use your real name.

Single player

Single player is entirely offline. Every file the game needs is bundled inside the app, so it makes no network requests at all while you play alone.

Multiplayer

Deathmatch is peer-to-peer. There is no game server of ours in the middle, and no match data is recorded anywhere. While a match is running, the following is sent directly to the other players in your room, and only to them:

  • the callsign you chose;
  • your position, aim direction and current weapon;
  • hit, death and score events.

To open a direct connection, two devices must first learn how to reach each other. This uses WebRTC, which means:

  • A signalling service introduces the two devices. By default this is the public PeerJS cloud service. It relays the introduction only; it does not see gameplay.
  • STUN servers are contacted to discover your network address.
  • Your IP address is, by the nature of a direct connection, visible to the other players in your room and to those services. This is unavoidable for peer-to-peer play and is the same for any game of this kind. If you would rather not share it, do not use multiplayer.

Room codes are short and are not secret. Anyone who knows or guesses a room code can join that match.

Permissions

The app requests internet and network-state access, and nothing else. It does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or identifiers, because it does not use them.

Children

OVERRUN is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal data, contact us and we will look into it — though as described above, we hold no data to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

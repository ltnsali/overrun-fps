OVERRUN

A fast arena shooter that runs in a browser and on Android from the same code. Hold an arena against waves of hostiles on your own, or share a room code and fight it out with other players. Around three megabytes, no download on first launch, and single player works with no connection at all.

  • Package name: com.overrun.fps
  • Free · no ads · no in-app purchases · no account
  • Android 6.0 and above, and any browser with WebGL
  • Rated PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen — moderate violence

Support and feedback

Email alialtunsu93@gmail.com with the name of the game, your device model and what you were doing when something went wrong. Bugs can also be raised as an issue on the repository, which is public.

Replies usually take up to three working days.

Privacy

The short version: single player sends nothing anywhere, and multiplayer sends your callsign, position and hits to the other players in your room and nowhere else. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no server of ours. The privacy policy sets out exactly what leaves your device, who can see it, and why.
